Water Sort — Privacy Policy

Water Sort: Color Puzzle (com.kirankotla.watersort)

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Short version: Water Sort has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location. Game progress stays on your device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers and usage data as described below.

1. Who we are

Water Sort (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a puzzle game developed by Kiran Kotla. Contact: kiran.kotla90@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect ourselves

None. We do not run our own servers and do not collect personal information.

3. Data stored on your device

The app saves your current level, coins, hints and sound / music / vibration settings locally on your device only. This data is removed when you uninstall the app.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner, interstitial, rewarded and app-open ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver, measure and prevent fraud in ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID and app-set ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • App interactions with ads, diagnostics and crash information

This data is transmitted over an encrypted connection and processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are shown a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) and can choose personalized or non-personalized ads. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings under Google → Ads.

5. Permissions

The app uses INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE to load ads, AD_ID for advertising, and VIBRATE for optional haptic feedback.

6. Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and data was collected by our ad partner, contact us and we will help.

7. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your data. Apart from the ad data processed by Google AdMob described above, no information is shared with third parties.

8. Your choices and deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored game data. For ad-related data you can reset your Advertising ID in device settings, or contact us at the address below.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this page and the “Last updated” date if this policy changes.

10. Contact

Email kiran.kotla90@gmail.com.
